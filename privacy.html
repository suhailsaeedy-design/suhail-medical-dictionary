Privacy Notice

This v21 clean rebuild stores the local account identifier, consent state, bookmarks, history, language, theme, and AI Study conversations/context in your browser on this device. Core Dictionary, Anatomy and Local Study functionality do not require a paid cloud database or AI provider.

Optional Google/Supabase authentication and cross-device sync can be configured separately. They are disabled by default. If the project owner enables sync and you connect a Google account, Selected Terms, Bookmarks, History, AI Study chats and interface preferences can be stored in your authenticated Supabase row. Local mode does not send this data to cloud services.

Educational medical content is for study and is not a replacement for a clinician or emergency service.

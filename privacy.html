Privacy Notice

This v21 clean rebuild stores the local account identifier, consent state, bookmarks, history, language, theme, and AI Study conversations/context in your browser on this device. Core Dictionary, Anatomy and Local Study functionality do not require a paid cloud database or AI provider.

Google/Supabase authentication and cross-device sync are configured for this deployment. If you connect a Google account, Selected Terms, Bookmarks, History, AI Study chats and interface preferences can be stored in your authenticated Supabase row. You can continue using local/offline study features without connecting Google; local-only data stays in this browser until you choose to sync it.

Educational medical content is for study and is not a replacement for a clinician or emergency service.

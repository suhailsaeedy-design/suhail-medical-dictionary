Educational Terms

Suhail Medical Dictionary is an educational reference for medical terminology. Definitions, explanations, translations, pronunciation, exports, and AI responses are provided for study and may contain errors.

Do not use the site as a substitute for qualified medical instruction, clinical judgment, diagnosis, emergency services, or individualized treatment. Important medical information should be verified using trusted textbooks, instructors, clinical guidelines, or other authoritative sources appropriate to the task.

Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials private. Automated abuse, attempts to access another user's account or chats, and attempts to bypass usage limits are not permitted.

AI and translation capacity is provided on a free-first basis and can temporarily stop when free provider limits are reached. The static/offline dictionary is designed to continue working independently of AI availability.

Medical Subject Headings (MeSH), when included, are sourced from the U.S. National Library of Medicine (NLM). Source/version notices must remain visible. NLM does not endorse this application.

Educational Terms

The conditions for using Suhail Medical Dictionary as an educational terminology and study reference.

Back to dictionaryPrivacy

Educational purpose

Suhail Medical Dictionary is an educational reference for medical terminology. Definitions, explanations, translations, pronunciation, exports, and AI responses are provided for study and may contain errors.

Not clinical care

Do not use the site as a substitute for qualified medical instruction, clinical judgment, diagnosis, emergency services, prescriptions, medication dosing, or individualized treatment. Important information should be verified using trusted academic or clinical sources appropriate to the task.

Account responsibility

Users are responsible for keeping account credentials private. Automated abuse, attempts to access another user’s account or chats, and attempts to bypass usage limits are not permitted.

AI availability

The bundled Study Engine is local and does not require a paid AI service or internet connection for source-bounded study modes. Optional free cloud AI or translation can become unavailable if a third-party free service changes or reaches its limits; core local study features continue independently.

Source attribution

Medical Subject Headings (MeSH), when included, are sourced from the U.S. National Library of Medicine (NLM). Source/version notices must remain visible. NLM does not endorse this application.

Educational notice

This project supports study and reference. It is not a medical diagnosis, emergency, or treatment service.

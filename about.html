About the creator

Suhail Saeedy · سهیل سیدي

Founder, Product Designer & Lead Developer · Suhail Medical Dictionary

Suhail Saeedy created Suhail Medical Dictionary as a practical medical-learning platform focused on fast terminology search, multilingual explanations, pronunciation, study exports, private account-based study history, and reliable online/offline access. The project is designed to make a large medical vocabulary easier to explore on both mobile and desktop.

Project Vision

Build a clear, modern medical terminology workspace that students can search, save, hear, print, export, and study without turning a large dictionary into a difficult interface.

🌐

Multilingual Access

The interface is designed for English, Pashto, Dari, Persian, Turkish, Arabic, and Chinese study workflows, with right-to-left content handled without breaking the overall layout.

⇩

Reliable Access

Dictionary packs and application files are designed for Progressive Web App use so students can keep useful terminology available after downloading it for offline study.

▦

Structured Medical Data

The production build imports U.S. National Library of Medicine MeSH terminology and organizes terms by searchable names, categories, definitions, synonyms, and study actions.

✦

Guided Study Workspace

The separate AI Study area is a learning feature for questions, comparisons, explanations, voice-assisted study, and selected-term context. It does not imply that the website itself was created by AI.

◉

Product Design Focus

The system prioritizes responsive layouts, clear navigation, modular components, multiple visual themes, accessible controls, and maintainable code so new features can be added without rebuilding the whole project.

Educational notice

Suhail Medical Dictionary is an educational medical-terminology project. It is not a replacement for a clinician, university curriculum, medical textbook, or emergency medical service. Important clinical facts should be verified with trusted academic and professional sources.

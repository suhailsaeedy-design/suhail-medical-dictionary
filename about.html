About the project

Suhail Medical Dictionary

An offline-first educational medical reference and study workspace combining a 1,158-term Dictionary, Clinical Reference, expanded schematic 3D Anatomy systems, Local AI Study and optional offline content packs.

v21

Clean, modular multilingual educational reference

This v21 rebuild intentionally avoids the old layered v16–v20 presentation stack. Each major module is kept separate and testable while sharing one consistent responsive shell.

What is included

  • Dictionary: 1,158 bundled medical terms across 17 categories.
  • Clinical Reference: 185 Conditions, 135 Procedures/Tests, 87 Pharmacology concepts and 7 educational calculators.
  • 3D Anatomy: 206 bones, 50 major muscles plus joints, ligaments, organs, nerves, vessels, 32 permanent teeth, eye and sinus structures in a simplified educational schematic viewer.
  • AI Study: Offline Explain, Compare, Quiz, Flashcards and Summary from bundled data.
  • PWA: Installable shell with separately manageable offline packs.

Medical-content boundary

This application is an educational reference. It is not a substitute for professional diagnosis, treatment, emergency care or medication-prescribing guidance. The bundled 3D geometry is schematic and should not be represented as a diagnostic or surgical-planning atlas.

Privacy & local data

Bookmarks, Selected Terms, History, interface preferences and local AI Study chats are account-scoped in this browser. Google/Supabase cross-device sync is configured for this deployment and only activates for a connected Google session. Cloud AI remains disabled unless an AI endpoint is explicitly configured.
